Context:
- Users have a `coaching_role` ('coach'|'coachee', migration 0018). The coaching reflection (POST /api/coaching/reflect) is mode-split by the logged-in user's coaching_role: coach → questions/patterns/next_questions; coachee → self-reflection points + action plan. Reflections are stored in `reflections` (id, session_id, transcript, questions, patterns, next_questions, mode, created_at) — but the table does NOT record WHO the coach is or WHO the coachee is, only the mode/perspective.
